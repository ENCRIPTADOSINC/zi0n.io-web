To evaluate the operational resilience of your equipment systematically, conduct these three primary verification routines.

### Controlled simulation of the Duress PIN and decoy session
The duress passcode serves as the primary barrier against physical coercion and forced unlock attempts. Lock your screen and enter your secondary Duress PIN instead of your master passcode. Confirm that the alternative workspace launches immediately: the user interface looks completely normal, yet your primary vaults and private keys are completely wiped from active RAM. ### Testing USB physical isolation and Cable Wipe triggers
The wired charging port represents a prime target for automated forensic extractors. Connect your locked handset to an unfamiliar computer using a standard USB cable. Verify that the data channel remains entirely dormant: no file transfer prompts should appear and ADB debugging bridges must remain unreachable. With Cable Wipe enabled, unauthorized connection attempts instantly sever data pins and purge volatile decryption tokens.

### Optical defense inspection with WipScreen
Spyware continuously attempts to record screen buffers to capture master passphrases upon display. Open a sensitive application, such as your encrypted notes vault or transaction signing suite. Attempt a manual screenshot using hardware buttons or project the display to an external monitor. WipScreen must immediately deny the action or produce a solid black screen, neutralizing optical data extraction.
