When safeguarding high-value digital assets, physical security alone cannot stop network-level tracking, and a typical commercial VPN cannot protect against a compromised mobile operating system. True operational resilience requires eliminating hardware attack vectors and network surveillance simultaneously.

## The critical flaws of fragmented mobile defenses

Mass-market consumer smartphones are designed for continuous user profiling. Even when running a commercial VPN, permanent hardware identifiers (IMEI numbers, wireless MAC addresses, and vendor advertising IDs) continue leaking in the background to hardware makers and cellular carriers.

Meanwhile, centralized VPN providers channel all customer traffic through single data centers subject to court orders, server seizures, and potential connection log leaks. Entrusting financial transactions to a single corporate proxy introduces an unacceptable single point of failure.
