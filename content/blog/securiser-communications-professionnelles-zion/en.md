## Critical threat vectors targeting mobile corporate communications

Conducting high-stakes corporate discussions on conventional consumer handsets exposes organizations to sophisticated espionage methodologies:

- **Cellular Interception and Rogue Towers (IMSI-Catchers):** Deployed near international conference venues, airports, or executive headquarters, fake cell towers force mobile devices into unencrypted legacy protocols to eavesdrop on voice calls and harvest metadata.
- **Zero-Click Ambient Eavesdropping Spyware (Pegasus, Predator):** Covert implants that penetrate mobile operating systems without requiring link clicks, turning onboard microphones into live listening bugs during closed-door deliberations.
- **Stealth Screen Scrapers and Keystroke Loggers:** Privileged background processes can mirror display buffers and log typed phrases prior to cryptographic packaging by messaging apps.
- **Identity Hijacking via SIM Swapping:** Unauthorized cellular provider transfers compromise SMS-based verification flows, allowing adversaries to seize corporate accounts on Signal, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
- **Physical Transit Extraction and Border Inspections:** Law enforcement or corporate espionage actors employing automated hardware extraction kits (Cellebrite, GrayKey) to dump device storage via physical cables.
