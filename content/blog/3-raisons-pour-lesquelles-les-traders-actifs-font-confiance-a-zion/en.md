## Critical threat vectors facing active mobile traders

Mobile devices have evolved into primary trading command centers, allowing investors to track order books, adjust leverage, and authorize blockchain transactions on the move. However, stock consumer operating systems present severe security loopholes:

- **Display snooping and clipboard manipulation:** Covert background spyware scans display buffers and system clipboards, replacing recipient wallet addresses on copy-paste commands and intercepting six-digit 2FA codes.
- **Telecom-driven SIM swapping:** Attackers leverage social engineering against carrier customer support representatives to hijack phone numbers and bypass SMS-based confirmations on major exchanges like Binance and Coinbase.
- **IP address tracking and denial of service:** Transacting across untrusted networks exposes your public IP address, allowing competitors or malicious actors to pinpoint your location or throttle connection bandwidth during high-volatility events.
- **Physical extortion and coercive device seizure:** During travel or public industry gatherings, being forced to unlock a standard phone hands complete control over unencrypted portfolios to malicious actors within seconds.
