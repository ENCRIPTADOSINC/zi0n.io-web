## The security gap between commercial phones and hardened devices

Standard mobile phones entrust data privacy to fragile userland sandboxes that skilled adversaries routinely bypass across multiple attack vectors:

- **Physical forensic extraction over USB:** Specialized law enforcement hardware (Cellebrite, GrayKey) targets unprotected USB controller pathways to brute-force locks and dump flash memory in raw format.
- **Stealth ambient microphone and camera surveillance:** Zero-click exploits like Pegasus gain low-level permissions, silently activating microphones and optical sensors during confidential briefings.
- **Screen scraping and seed phrase theft:** Malicious background services capture video frames and clipboard buffers whenever recovery phrases, authentication codes, or financial dashboards appear on display.
- **Cellular exploitation and SIM swapping:** Physical carrier SIMs remain vulnerable to carrier insider fraud, identity theft, and rogue cell towers (IMSI-catchers).
- **Physical coercion and forced unlocking:** Standard devices provide zero recourse during an extortion scenario where an individual is forced to enter their passcode under physical threat.
