Consumer smartphones are built to harvest telemetry and serve targeted advertising. When used to manage digital assets, standard mobile devices introduce severe attack surfaces:

- **Silent clipboard eavesdropping:** Mobile Trojans monitor system memory to swap your recipient address with an attacker address the instant you paste.

- **Physical extortion and coercive inspections:** When facing robbery, unlawful searches, or high-risk international checkpoints, being forced to unlock a standard phone exposes your entire net worth immediately.
