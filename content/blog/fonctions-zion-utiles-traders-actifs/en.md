Active cryptocurrency traders execute high-stakes orders across decentralized and centralized platforms directly from mobile devices. ## Critical attack vectors targeting mobile traders

Using an off-the-shelf commercial Android or iOS device exposes active traders to significant attack surfaces:

- **SIM swapping and 2FA interception:** Threat actors leverage social engineering against carrier support staff to transfer the victim's phone number, instantly receiving SMS verification codes to drain exchange accounts.
- **IP correlation and network profiling:** Unencrypted mobile connections and public Wi-Fi expose connection metadata, allowing adversaries to tie specific blockchain transactions to physical identities and physical coordinates.
- **Invisible keyloggers and screen capture malware:** Mobile spyware can operate undetected in the background, logging passcodes, recording chart setups, and capturing private seed phrases during wallet generation.
- **Physical extortion and device robbery ("5-dollar wrench attack"):** When an investor is physically compelled to unlock their smartphone, traditional biometric and alphanumeric locks become useless.
- **USB forensic extraction:** During border inspections, device confiscation, or hardware theft, forensic extraction tools such as Cellebrite and GrayKey attempt to extract unencrypted memory dumps via physical cables.
